# Code of Conduct

This project should be a practical and respectful place to collaborate.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct, kind, and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but respond to impact when someone points it out.
- Keep criticism focused on the work and the technical tradeoffs.
- Respect maintainers' decisions about scope, priorities, and release timing.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Repeated off-topic arguments after a maintainer has closed a discussion.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Abuse of issue, discussion, or review channels.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, close issues, restrict participation, or block users when behavior harms the project. Security-sensitive or private reports should use the security process in `SECURITY.md`.
